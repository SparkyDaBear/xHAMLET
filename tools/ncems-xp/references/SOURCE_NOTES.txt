The following documents supplied the reference equations, coefficients, and distance method for XP v3.3. Their hashes identify the original copies. Local copies may be placed in this directory.
1. Sitarik_ED_Table7.docx: supplied XP equations 8 and 9.
2. Sitarik_ED_Table8.docx: six supplied residue-pair coefficient sets.
3. zjw2491.pdf: Bullock et al. (2016), Molecular & Cellular Proteomics15:2491-2500,
   DOI10.1074/mcp.M116.058560. This is the original MNXL paper.

These documents are not a universal calibration table for all amino-acid pairs
or all crosslinkers. The v3.3 policy uses source scope and chemistry gates.
The six supplied coefficients were reconciled with the 2025 PGK paper's
Supplementary TableS2, DOI10.1126/sciadv.ads7379. Conditional/source-specific
contexts remain exploratory rather than universal chemistry claims.

DSS/BS3 K-K uses the published MNXL positive distribution with an explicitly
labelled zero-cutoff XP adaptation. The original negative penalty and ancillary
counts are separate. Unparameterized candidates can retain a common-reference
geometry diagnostic, which is NOT their calibrated chemistry-specific XP.

Active parameter and chemistry sources are in data_v33/source_catalog.json.
The installed NCLEdetector/Jwalk source remains external and unchanged. Its
original authors/license must be acknowledged for the actual methods used.
